How it works

Simple by design.

One reveal a day, perfectly calibrated to you.

01
Set your preferences
Tell Feedz your budget, cooking time, portion sizes and dietary needs. Complete the algorithm-setter by picking your top two meals each round.
02
The daily reveal
Once a day, at the same time for all users, your dinner is revealed. One option. Chosen for you. Just like that.
03
Sunday shopping list
Every Sunday, your week's shopping list is generated automatically — ready before the weekend ends.
04
Share with friends
Optionally share your meal. Like and save friends' recipes — they fold into your own algorithm over the next two weeks.
Features

Built around
how you actually eat.

One option. On purpose.
Choice fatigue is a genuine drain. Feedz eliminates it — one meal, perfectly tailored, every day. No second-guessing required.
A learning algorithm
Your preferences, cooking habits, and saved recipes are woven together so each suggestion gets sharper over time.
Friends page
See what your friends are eating. Like and save their meals — saved recipes feed back into your algorithm within two weeks.
Auto shopping list
Your full week of ingredients, compiled automatically every Sunday. Everything you need, nothing you don't.
Themed weeks
Movie Week, Around the World, Eco Week — Feedz turns dinner into an event, with facts and stories to match each theme.
Feedz Wrapped
